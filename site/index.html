Dogfighterfor Apple Silicon Macs

Airfix Dogfighter is a Windows game from 2000. Model planes fight through a house. This project runs it in a sharp Retina window on a Mac with M1 or later. It was tested on an M4 Max. You bring your own original disc.

Desk dogfight live

Two toy planes fly at the game's own speeds and turn rates. Stray shots break glass on the sill, and broken glass drops a star.

Before you start

Four things to have ready.

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon

    An M1 or any later chip.

  • macOS 26.4 or later works best

    It uses Mach9. That is Wine made for Apple Silicon. Flight runs at about 100 frames a second on the test Mac. Older macOS uses Rosetta 2 and gets about 30.

  • Your own game disc

    The original CD and a CD drive to copy it. A BIN/CUE copy you made earlier also works. Be aware that a regular ISO won't work. It leaves out the music. Make a full BIN/CUE copy of the disc instead.

  • About 6.5 GB of free space

    That holds the game and its sharper textures and menus. You also need internet while you install.

Assembly 3 steps

Three steps in Terminal. The README has the details. It also says what to do when a step fails.

  1. 1

    Copy your disc to a file

    Install cdrdao with Homebrew, put the disc in the drive and copy it to Dogfighter.bin.

    brew install cdrdao
cdrdao read-cd --read-raw --datafile Dogfighter.bin --driver generic-mmc:0x20000 Dogfighter.toc
  2. 2

    Get the project and run setup

    Setup downloads what it needs and checks each download. It copies the game and its music from your disc file. Then it makes the sharper textures and menus. The second line installs Rosetta 2. Skip it on macOS 26.4 or later.

    xcode-select --install
softwareupdate --install-rosetta --agree-to-license
git clone https://github.com/natemate90/dogfighter.git
cd dogfighter
./setup.sh ../Dogfighter.bin
  3. 3

    Play

    Start the game from Terminal. Or double-click Dogfighter.app. It opens in a normal Mac window you can resize. Ctrl+Option frees the mouse.

    ./dogfighter

    Cleared for take-off

Before and after made on your Mac

The game starts with the best picture by itself. Your Mac makes the sharper pictures once, from your own game files. Setup also downloads some reference photos once. Nothing of yours is uploaded. --original turns it all off.

Smooth edges

Jagged edges of 3D shapes are smoothed, with 4 samples per pixel. The 3D picture uses every pixel of a Retina screen.

Sharp in the distance

Floors and walls stay sharp far away, with 16x filtering. On the test Mac the frame rate stayed the same.

Four times the pixels

The menus, loading screens, mission books, medals and pilot photos get four times the pixels. The textures of the 3D world get twice. Setup makes them on your Mac.

Letters like the original

The letters on the pilot screens, in the mission book and in the briefing are drawn again from matching fonts. Their strokes keep the weight and soft grey of the original. The hand-painted signs on the main menu are drawn again from the game's own pixels.

A window that keeps its shape

The game opens in a normal Mac window. Drag an edge or click the green button. The window keeps the picture's shape, so nothing is stretched.

A pointer that follows your mouse

The menu pointer now moves as far as the Mac pointer. Before, it went four times as far. Its speed follows your Mac's mouse setting.

Sharper intro videos

Optional. The two intro videos can be made four times as large, at 60 frames a second. That is a long run with about 10 GB of downloads. It is still being tested.

A cooler, quieter Mac

On a ProMotion screen the game draws 120 pictures a second. Start it with --fps=60 and it draws at most 60. The Mac stays cooler.

What else the kit adds

On top of the sharper picture you get this.

A Speed
About 100 frames a second in flight on macOS 26.4 or later. At full Retina size it measured 62 to 89. Missions load in 2 to 5 seconds. Measured on an M4 Max.
B Controllers
Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro controllers and USB joysticks should work. The launcher turns Joystick on when one is connected. Nobody has tried a real one yet.
C Music
The music comes from the disc's audio tracks. Setup copies them, so you play without the disc.
D Friends
Fly against each other over the internet. Use Tailscale and skip the router setup. --join=ADDRESS adds a friend's game to the Address book.
E Saves and backups
--backup puts your pilots and settings in a dated zip file. --restore puts the newest one back.
F Self-test
--doctor checks the install without starting the game. It prints a fix for each problem and lists your controllers.

Choose a finish

Add one of these after ./dogfighter. The README lists them all.

  • ./dogfighter

    The best picture, by default.

  • --original

    The game as it looked in 2000.

  • --fps=60

    At most 60 pictures a second.

  • --mute

    No sound. Only for this start.

  • --no-hd-textures

    The game's own textures, still at full Retina size.

  • --doctor

    Checks the install and prints a fix for each problem.

  • --backup

    Puts your pilots and settings in a dated zip file.

  • --restore

    Puts the newest backup back.
